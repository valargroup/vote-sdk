## key_decisions
- **DC share secrets and the per-action recovery hint derive from an account-scoped proxy recovery key (ARK). The ARK comes from the account's Orchard external OVK, per (network, round). A hotkey-derived fallback covers hosts without an account key.** — With an account key, the delegator can recover (delegate, amount, layout), rebuild the DC helper payloads and share nullifiers, and verify its delegation after losing both the DB and the hotkey. The pool weight is final and safe on chain, so verifiability should survive hotkey loss. Vizor holds a UFVK for software, Keystone and Ledger accounts. (rejected: Hotkey-only derivation: the per-round random hotkey is unrecoverable (vizor dossier), so this loses verification and share redelivery after reinstall. An off-chain backup: Vizor has none. Brute force over w and d: infeasible for large W (up to 2^30 ballots) times the registry size.)
- **Each DC action carries a fixed 32-byte recovery_hint, bound by the batch sighash. It is ChaCha20-Poly1305 under a key derived from (ARK, van_nullifier), encrypting (version, layout, delegate_index, ballots).** — This is the only cheap, deterministic way to re-learn the hidden (d, w) after DB loss. It is self-authenticating: the recomputed DC must equal the on-chain value. It is constant-size and indistinguishable from random, and the chain treats it as opaque. (rejected: Plaintext (d, w) in the tx, which breaks B2's hiding of delegate and amount (the B1 weakness). No hint, which leaves no recovery. Storing the plan in the memo of the ZKP1 output, which the hardware wallet displays and which the hotkey, not the account, decrypts.)
- **dc_seed binds (van_nullifier, delegate_index, ballots, layout). Share randomness, blinds, the denomination split and the shuffle derive from dc_seed with new domains, in a voting-circuits helper.** — A DC rebuilt with a changed plan never reuses El Gamal nonces. This mirrors the existing split between standard and single-share domains (vc:src/vote_proof/builder.rs:538-566), and keeps derivation reproducible by third parties. (rejected: Binding only the VAN, as ZKP2 does: a crash, a plan change and a rebuild on the same VAN would reuse nonces with different plaintexts. Keying shares on the hotkey sk: this loses ARK-only recovery.)
- **The delegate key is Ed25519. Software accounts derive it with ZIP-32 registered derivation, path (coin_type', account', key_index' with tag zvote-delegate-key-ed25519-v1) under the voting ZIP number. Hardware-only accounts use a random key with a mandatory bech32m backup (HRP zvdksec).** — Registered derivation is the ZIP-32 mechanism designed for protocol keys. Ad-hoc derivation is documented as deprecated and NOT RECOMMENDED for new protocols (zip32 arbitrary.rs:1-13). Ed25519 is already a dependency, is verifiable natively in Go and by any off-chain tool, and matches the identity research. key_index gives rotation. (rejected: Ad-hoc ZIP-32 derivation (deprecated; it would force a key migration later). The raw BIP-39 seed as a hotkey seed (it would equal the real account key). A RedPallas DK (more tooling friction for verifiers and explorers). Proving knowledge of an Orchard address (unnecessary in B2, where delegates never receive VANs).)
- **Proxy allocation is a separate durable record (proxy_allocations and proxy_allocation_entries), with its own lifecycle: preview, then commit with a plan_digest, then locked. The planner plans ZKP1 for bundles with slots even with zero ballot intents, and plans casts only for remainder weight.** — Proxy delegation must work with zero self-votes. Today ZKP1 is planned only for bundles with due casts (classify.rs:397, 513-555), and casts need a terminal ballot (classify.rs:211). The digest guarantees that what the user confirmed is what gets executed. (rejected: Encoding proxy as pseudo ballot intents (proposal 0): intents are per proposal, not per bundle, and the roster rules would reject them. Implicit commits: the plan could drift between preview and proving.)
- **The planner uses Hamilton largest remainder in basis points, then a minimum-1-ballot bump funded by Keep or the largest delegate. Assignment uses an exact split-free search (B ≤ 4, budget 2^20), then best-fit decreasing, then greedy splitting. The lexicographic objective is fewest DCs, then fewest bundles touched.** — Integer ballots stay within 1 of each quota. The guaranteed bound is #DC ≤ D + B − 1, with #DC = D whenever a split-free packing exists. Untouched keep-only bundles need no tx. The prototype verified these properties on about 12k random cases. (rejected: Applying percentages per bundle (up to D·B DCs and compounding rounding error). D'Hondt or Sainte-Laguë (biased toward large or small parties, less intuitive). Optimal integer programming on mobile (unnecessary).)
- **DCs are always ordered before casts in one per-bundle submission. The submission is combined with ZKP1 when the bundle's authorization is unconfirmed. A 700 KB JSON packer defers tail casts to a follow-up MsgCastVoteBatch.** — ZKP4 requires MAX authority. Combining avoids a separate timing event. ZKP1, 10 ZKP4 and 50 ZKP2 together would be about 0.94 MB against the roughly 1 MB effective REST/Comet cap. (rejected: Separate proxy-only txs always (extra timing event). Splitting DCs across txs (more linkage). One mega-batch (exceeds the cap).)
- **Persistence uses new proxy tables (proxy_slots, proxy_helper_share_plans, proxy_share_deliveries, proxy_recovery_cursor) and two new chain_submissions kinds (proxy_cast_batch, delegate_proxy_cast_batch). ShareKey gains a CommitmentRef::{Vote, Proxy} variant.** — The existing share tables have foreign keys to votes and triggers keyed on vote generations. proposal_id is validated as 1..50 in the vote paths (vote.rs:4498-4499). A schema version bump makes older SDKs refuse the DB. (rejected: Virtual proposal ids for slots (silently breaks validators and the assumption that key == payload). Adding a commitment_kind column to share_delegations (bigger blast radius on heavily tested tables).)
- **Proxy gating sits outside vote_protocol. It uses a WalletCapabilities.proxy_delegation list, an optional top-level dynamic-config object whose version mismatch disables only the feature, and a per-round proxy extension signed by trusted_keys under the new domain zcash-shielded-vote:round-proxy:v1. A proxy fingerprint change maps to SameChainServiceUpdate.** — Bumping vote_protocol makes every client without that version reject the whole config (config/mod.rs:1004-1008), which would force Zodl to upgrade. A ProtocolChanged decision would discard cached voting state mid-round. The new domain prevents cross-protocol signature reuse. (rejected: A vote_protocol v2 bump. Putting the proxy version in SupportedVersions (it feeds ProtocolChanged). Editing the pinned static config in place (breaks Zodl's sha256 pin). Signing the directory with trusted_keys (those keys can also sign rounds).)
- **Registry trust model: the chain registry is authoritative for delegate_index, DK and status. The signed directory snapshot (purpose-separated directory_keys, threshold-capable) carries display data only. Verifier attestations are checked client-side. The selected delegates are cross-checked against the chain at preview, at commit and immediately before proving.** — A DC hides d, so the chain cannot reject a bad index at submission time, and a wrong index burns weight. A lying REST node or directory alone cannot redirect a delegation. (rejected: Trusting the snapshot alone. Pinning a per-round snapshot hash in the round entry, which freezes mid-round registrations and adds no security once chain cross-checks exist.)
- **Lookalike and confusable detection, key fingerprints and search ranking live in the Rust library. Vizor only renders.** — One tested implementation across five platforms. The rules also bear on security (impersonation). (rejected: A Vizor-only Dart implementation (duplicated, harder to test against vectors).)

## baseline_challenges
- HIGH: B2 gives no way to recover (delegate_index, w) after the local DB is lost. The DC hides d and the successor VAN hides w, so after a reinstall the delegator cannot show its delegations, recompute DC share nullifiers (blinds are derived secrets), redeliver DC shares, or resume voting with the remainder (W − Σw unknown). Brute force is infeasible for large W. Fix: a fixed 32-byte, sighash-bound recovery_hint per DC action, AEAD under a key derived from an account-scoped recovery key (ARK from the Orchard OVK) and van_nullifier. DC share secrets derive from the same ARK plus (van_nullifier, d, w, layout). Add a per-round proxy-action feed (chain REST or an untrusted indexer, since hints are self-authenticating), and optionally nullifier→tx lookups for gov and VAN nullifiers.
- MEDIUM-HIGH: "Unchanged ZKP3" stops holding once private-vote V2 lands. The V2 branch rewrites share_reveal in place to the vector, DOMAIN_VC_V2 form (vc-pv:src/share_reveal/circuit.rs:1-30; about 540 lines changed) and trims the scalar El Gamal gadget. Fix: keep the current scalar ZKP3 as a separately named circuit (share_reveal_scalar), with its VK still verified on chain for proposal_id == 0. ZKP4 keeps its own scalar El Gamal gadget. Helpers keep both provers and route proposal 0 to the scalar one. DCs stay V1-domain commitments, and the client always emits V1-shaped DC share payloads.
- MEDIUM: ZKP3 itself accepts proposal 0, but three validators reject it today. The helper's validatePayload requires proposal_id 1..50 and vote_decision < 8 (sdk:internal/helper/api.go:773-779). The chain's RevealShare ValidateBasic has the same rule. The client's vote-recovery and share paths validate proposal 1..50 and the decision against num_options (zv:src/vote.rs:4498-4499). Fix: explicit proposal-0 branches. The helper and the chain validate vote_decision as a registered delegate index. The client uses a separate proxy recovery format and share path.
- MEDIUM: one atomic tx of ZKP1 + 10 ZKP4 + 50 ZKP2 is about 0.94 MB of JSON, above the roughly 1 MB effective REST/Comet cap; a 50-vote delegate-and-cast is already about 78% of it. Fix: the chain publishes max_proxy_actions_per_batch (10), a maximum number of mixed actions and a byte cap in ProtocolCapabilities. The client packer keeps ZKP1 and all DCs and defers tail casts to a follow-up MsgCastVoteBatch (default budget 700 KB).
- MEDIUM: delegate validity cannot be enforced when a DC is submitted, because d is hidden until reveal. A DC to an unregistered or retired index silently burns weight. Fix: the client makes mandatory chain cross-checks at preview, commit and pre-prove. Registry indices are append-only and never reused. The chain accepts reveals for any index registered before the round's vote_end_time, and retirement affects only future rounds. Helpers reject unknown indices with a permanent, distinguishable error.
- LOW-MEDIUM: per-delegate delegation counts are publicly derivable. The client posts all 16 shares per standard DC (zv:src/share.rs:1002-1006), so reveals on (0, d) divided by 16 approximates the number of delegations per delegate. The number of DC actions per tx is also visible. Totals stay hidden, so decision 4 holds literally, but this side channel should be documented. Vizor should not display counts.
- LOW: with w = W the zero-weight successor VAN is still appended, because the chain appends the final VAN first (sdk:x/vote/keeper/msg_server.go:221-269). Harmless, but the client must treat it as dead weight and never cast with it.
- CONFIG (affects Zodl): baseline gating must not touch supported_versions.vote_protocol, since one unsupported advertised version rejects the whole config (zv:src/config/mod.rs:1004-1008). It must not edit the sha256-pinned static config in place. Publish directory and verifier keys in a new static config at a new URL, and put the proxy version in a separate object, not SupportedVersions, so its addition does not trigger ProtocolChanged and discard cached voting state.

## cross_component_contracts
- REQUIRES from CIRCUITS (C1): a ZKP4 circuit inside voting-circuits. ## open_questions_for_owner
- 1. Should viewing-key (UFVK) holders be able to see an account's proxy delegations and their amounts? This is a consequence of keying recovery hints and DC secrets to an account-derived key, and it is what lets delegators verify after losing the hotkey. Recommendation: yes. It matches viewing-key semantics.
- 2. In proxy-only rounds (no self-votes), should one DC share be submitted immediately (early "your delegation is counted" confirmation) at the cost of a timing link between that tx and the delegate? Recommendation: yes, matching today's behavior for votes. Keep the existing vote designation whenever the round has a vote.
- 3. Should delegates be able to record an explicit "abstain" route, so delegators see "abstained" rather than "did not vote"? The tally effect is identical. Recommendation: yes, using the sentinel decision 0xFFFFFFFF.
- 4. May a delegator add delegates after its first proxy batch, using remaining weight from bundles that have not self-voted yet? Recommendation: not in v1. One committed allocation per round. Add append-only top-ups in v1.1.
- 5. What is the minimum per delegate? Recommendation: the protocol floor is 1 ballot (0.125 ZEC). Allow a per-round min_ballots_per_delegate in the signed round proxy config to limit dust, since each DC costs 1 proof plus 16 reveals regardless of size.
- 6. Request a ZIP number for the shielded-voting ZIP now, so software delegate keys can use ZIP-32 registered derivation from the seed. Recommendation: request it. If it is not assigned before launch, ship random keys with backup for every account rather than deprecated ad-hoc derivation.
- 7. Accept that the approximate number of delegations per delegate is publicly derivable (reveal count divided by 16), alongside hidden totals? Recommendation: accept and document. Vizor does not display counts. Revisit if counts become a coercion or market signal.
- 8. Cap of 10 delegates: is it per wallet per round across all bundles, or per bundle? Recommendation: per wallet (account) per round across bundles. It is a UX cap, not a protocol invariant.

## risks
- The effective-VAN-weight refactor is cross-cutting. About a dozen files read bundles.total_note_value to recompute VANs or build ZKP2, including load_van_tree_entries (zv:src/storage/queries/mod.rs:2335) and tree-sync leaf verification (zv:src/tree_sync.rs:506-545). Missing one site breaks remainder voting or makes tree sync fail on the post-DC leaf.
- Generalizing ShareKey and share tracking touches the most heavily tested subsystem (share_tracking tests total thousands of lines). Regressions there would delay or lose vote shares, not only DC shares.
- If client and chain sighash encodings diverge, every proxy tx is rejected. Frozen cross-language vectors are mandatory before activation.
- The REST and Comet size caps are close to existing batches. If the chain does not publish byte caps, the packer budget (700 KB) is a guess, and private-vote V2 (about 65 KB per vote) would shrink feasible batches drastically.
- Mobile proving and foreground time: ZKP1, up to 10 ZKP4 and casts on mobile are unmeasured. There are no mobile numbers in the repos. Mobile work is foreground-only with a 15-minute auto-lock.
- The recovery feed is a new dependency. If neither chain REST nor an indexer serves it, R1 and R2 recovery degrade to impossible for (d, w).
- The ZIP number for registered derivation may not arrive in time. Falling back to random keys for everyone means software delegates must also back up a separate secret.
- Lookalike detection is heuristic. The UTS #39 tables add a dependency, and impersonation through display names or pfps remains possible. The mandatory chain cross-check protects the target, but not the user's judgment.
- Helper and chain capacity: every DC adds 16 reveals against the 256-reveals-per-block cap and about 0.58 shares/s per helper worker. Late-round DC surges could miss vote_end_time.
- The planner's interaction with existing holds (combined-cast rejection streaks, imported-capability barrier, retirement) adds new obligation combinations. Exhaustive-match discipline helps, but the state space grows.
- X API and verifier availability affect delegate onboarding, not delegator safety, but may stall the directory.
- Schema v25 is one-way. A Vizor rollback to an older SDK cannot open the DB, which the existing newer-version guard refuses by design.
