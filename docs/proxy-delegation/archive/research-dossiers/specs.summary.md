## key_facts
- No ZIP normatively specifies proxy delegation. Both protocol drafts only say the send-based VAN model enables a future 'VAN-to-VAN delegation proof that consumes one VAN and produces two with subdivided num_ballots' (ZIP-A draft-valargroup-shielded-voting__adam_voting-protocol-client-delay.md:342-345,1522-1533,1613-1618; ZIP-G ..._greg_shielded-voting-fixes.md:400-403,1460-1471,1598-1603).
- Only ZIP-G has the requirement 'The protocol supports delegation of voting authority to a third-party hotkey' (ZIP-G:213-214). ZIP-A dropped it but keeps 'The Delegation Proof does not constrain the hotkey address to match the holder's key' (ZIP-A:495-499).
- The two drafts conflict on hotkey generation: ZIP-A says 'MUST be derived … fresh BIP 39 mnemonic … MUST NOT be exported or backed up; needed only for the duration of the voting round' (ZIP-A:501-508); ZIP-G says 'MAY … or sampled randomly … an application concern' (ZIP-G:553-557).
- The book's VAN split consumes the delegator's VAN and outputs two VANs (delegate amount plus remainder) with the same bitmask. Multiple delegates are done by successive transactions, and delegates may 'further delegate' (shielded-vote-book/delegation/delegation-setup.md:3-24; partial-delegation.md:23-30; userflow/delegating-your-vote.md:20-26).
- The book says delegation 'is specified as an optional extension (§6.0 in Gov Steps V1)' (delegation-setup.md:34; partial-delegation.md:38). Gov Steps V1 is an external baseline also cited by voting-circuits/src/vote_proof/README.md:241. It is not in any provided repository.
- Terminology already presumes a VAN-consuming delegation that is never specified: 'VAN nullifier … published when the VAN is consumed (to cast a vote or delegate)' (ZIP-A:86-90,329-330; ZIP-G:86,389). The setup ZIP says 'VAN nullifiers (from delegation consumption)' (SETUP:141-142).
- ZKP2 makes num_ballots a private witness constrained by sum(shares) = num_ballots (ZIP-A:816,900-902), so a delegate necessarily learns the exact delegated ballot count. The book contradicts itself: 'Nobody can observe the delegation amounts' (partial-delegation.md:34) vs 'No one but the delegation recipient can see the delegation amount' (overview/design-principles.md:20).
- ZKP2 Condition 7: 'The new VAN MUST reuse the old VAN's diversified address and commitment randomness' (ZIP-A:895-896). The rationale says this is safe because 'the shared fields are never externally observable' (ZIP-A:1470-1480).
- VAN nullifier = Poseidon(vsk.nk, 'vote authority spend', round, van) is keyed by the hotkey owner's vsk.nk (ZIP-A:332-336), so a delegator cannot compute the delegate's VAN nullifier.
- No ZIP or book page claims coercion resistance, receipt-freeness or vote-buying resistance (grep across all specs). Per-vote secrets are deterministic from the hotkey seed (ZIP-A:520-558), so voters can reproduce and reveal VC openings.
- The only voter-verifiability features specified are transaction status polling (WAPI:752-757), share-status by share nullifier (WAPI:669-693) and the aggregate tally check (ZIP-A:210-211; SETUP:358-376). SUB says 'Voters currently have no privacy-preserving way to confirm that their shares were submitted' (SUB:542-548).
- Keystone signs the ZIP 244 sighash of a single-action governance PCZT. It displays Amount 0.00000001 ZEC, Fee 0, To {governance hotkey address}, and a memo 'I am authorizing this hotkey … to vote on {round_name} with {amount}.{frac} ZEC' (KS draft-valargroup-keystone-voting.md:283-302). The memo 'does not enter the ZKP circuit' (KS:110-112). Keystone is not needed again after delegation (KS:332-335).
- [CODE] The chain recomputes the delegation sighash from tx1_effects and binds the TX1 action's rk, nullifier and cmx_new to the proof (vote-sdk x/vote/ante/validate.go:259-272; ffi/tx1/effects.go:38-62). The circuit derives the VAN vpk from the output note's address (voting-circuits/src/delegation/README.md:311-323). So the Keystone 'To' address is bound to the VAN hotkey. ZIP-A:740-749 and WAPI:541-547 (client-provided sighash, not recomputed) are stale.
- [CODE] TX1 effects are 1 + 1×820 bytes with ActionCount = 1 (vote-sdk ffi/tx1/effects.go:11-17) and are posted on-chain as MsgDelegateVote.tx1_effects (proto/svote/v1/tx.proto:68). [INF] 820 bytes matches a full Orchard action including the 580-byte enc_ciphertext, so the memo is on-chain and encrypted to the hotkey address.
- [CODE] Proposal authority is 51 bits with 50 proposals (voting-circuits/src/params.rs:30-44; vote_proof/README.md:241-250; vote-sdk x/vote/types/keys.go:48). Every ZIP and the book still say 16 bits with 15 proposals (ZIP-A:321-325; ZIP-G:381-385; SETUP:318-320; WAPI:271-273; book data-types.md:20).
- [CODE] The circuit requires the signed note value to be exactly 1 zatoshi (voting-circuits/src/delegation/README.md:18-19,177-178). The ZIPs say 0 in the circuit (ZIP-A:631,664-665; KS:38-39,199-203); the book says 1 (zkps/zkp1-delegation-proof.md:25).
- The book claims vsk.nk and nk are 'the same field element … not distinct key material' (data-types.md:36; circuits/van-nullifier.md:16,23-25). This contradicts ZIP-A:1355-1366 and is impossible when a third party's hotkey holds the VAN.
- VCT depth is 24 (about 16.7M leaves) per round. Each vote adds 2 leaves (new VAN plus VC) and the capacity rationale assumes about 1M leaves (ZIP-A:430-445,1557-1572).
- Submission server: each share goes to exactly ceil(s/2) servers (SUB:332-342). The last-moment buffer is min(10% of the round, 3600 s), inside which a single full-amount share is submitted immediately (SUB:294-312). Each share needs an expensive ZKP3 (SUB:316-324).
- WAPI and SUB contradict each other on submit_at = 0: SUB says submit immediately (SUB:264-265); WAPI says 'submit at the last possible moment before vote_end_time' (WAPI:635). SUB's equal-split decomposition (SUB:534-541) contradicts the book's base-10 denomination scheme (appendices/share-splits.md).
- [DOC/CODE] The custody handoff uses a round-bound VotingHotkeyTargetV1 (chain id, network, round, address index 0, 43-byte raw Orchard address). The VAN opening, including van_comm_rand, is delivered off-chain, and there is no public-chain recovery (zcash_voting/docs/exporting-to-external-software.md:41-49,80-85,175-195).
- The setup ZIP's round parameters have no delegation flags or cutoff (SETUP:313-326). The round ID is a Poseidon hash of fixed fields (SETUP:328-331; ZIP-G:312-354). Service discovery is an admin-approved centralized /api/voting-config directory (SETUP:271-292). [CODE] Wallets actually consume a signed static/dynamic config with per-round RoundEntry{auth_version, ea_pk, signatures} (zcash_voting/zcash_voting/src/config/mod.rs:855-892).

## design_implications
- Treat proxy delegation as a new hotkey-signed chain transaction (a VAN split) that runs after normal ZKP1 registration. This matches the only spec direction (ZIP-A:1522-1533, 1613-1618), keeps Keystone untouched (KS:332-335, 122-123), and adds no user-facing hardware signatures (ZIP-A:204-205).
- The split proof must spend the input VAN with exactly ZKP2's VAN nullifier derivation and tag (ZIP-A:332), into the existing VAN nullifier set. A separate tag would let the same VAN be both voted and split.
- Split constraints: per-output range checks (each output below 2^30, and at least 1 if zero outputs are disallowed) so sum conservation cannot wrap the field. Copy the proposal_authority bitmask (51 bits, per code) by equality to every output, never reset it to MAX_PROPOSAL_AUTHORITY. Reuse ZKP2 Conditions 1-5 for spending.
- Choose the arity: book-style iterated 1→2 (10 delegates means 10 sequential transactions with confirmations and resyncs) or a single 1→(k+1), k ≤ 10, proof with padding. An atomic register-and-split message modelled on MsgDelegateAndCastVoteBatch's synthetic-root chaining (vote-sdk x/vote/ante/validate.go:226-240) avoids a wait between ZKP1 and the split, and reduces timing linkage.
- Decide explicitly whether delegators may observe delegate usage. Keeping ZIP-A:895-896 (reuse gov_comm_rand) plus delegator-chosen output randomness lets delegators see that, when and on which proposals the delegate voted, but not how. Preventing that needs delegate-side re-randomization (a ZKP2 change) and rewriting ZIP-A:1470-1480.
- Answer to the user's open question at spec level: 'whether and on which proposals' is cheaply observable by the delegator if it knows the VAN opening. 'How they voted' needs delegate cooperation: a receipt revealing (shares_hash, vote_decision) for the VC in the same transaction, checked against vc = Poseidon(DOMAIN_VC, round, shares_hash, proposal_id, decision) (ZIP-A:351). Optionally the delegate also reveals blinds so delegators can check share-status (WAPI:669-693).
- Define how delegated VANs are delivered. Today only an off-chain authenticated channel exists (custody handoff). To avoid revealing the delegator's identity to an influencer, and to give delegates chain-based recovery, add an in-band ciphertext of the VAN opening encrypted to the delegate's Orchard address, trial-decryptable like the existing tx1_effects enc_ciphertext. The delegate self-verifies it by recomputing the VAN commitment.
- Introduce a delegate key profile separate from today's round-only, non-backed-up hotkey (ZIP-A:501-508). Either a stable, backed-up delegate hotkey, or a long-lived identity key that signs a per-round VotingHotkeyTargetV1-style target that the X/ICNS-style directory points to. Spec text for the hotkey section and for Keystone (KS:107-109) must change.
- Delegates need multi-VAN tooling: V delegated VANs × P proposals of ZKP2 plus up to 16 shares each. Consider a VAN-merge (join) circuit, accepting that it creates large-weight VANs in high denomination bands. Consider a per-round delegation cutoff earlier than vote_end_time − last_moment_buffer, so delegates do not fall into single-share mode (SUB:294-312).
- Ballot granularity: weights are whole ballots (0.125 ZEC). Percentages need a rounding rule. 'Up to 10 delegates with ≥1 ballot each' needs ≥10 ballots in the VAN being split. Multi-bundle holders have one VAN per bundle (KS:158-163), so splitting each bundle's VAN or merging first must be designed.
- Proposals already voted before a split are cleared bits that the delegate inherits. Vizor must show delegates which proposals each delegated VAN can still vote on. Per-proposal delegation (disjoint bitmasks at full weight) is expressible by the data model if wanted.
- Rollout: the split circuit's verification key must be in round verification_keys (SETUP:325-326), so the feature is per-round from creation. A vote_protocol bump makes non-upgraded wallets MUST-reject the config (WAPI:719-722), so plan a capability-style flag. Any per-round delegation flag or directory pointer belongs in the signed dynamic config's per-round entry (new auth_version) or a separate signed document, not in WAPI's stale v1 schema.
- Before extending the specs, reconcile the drift: 51-bit authority, tx1_effects sighash, 1-zatoshi signed note, batch messages, signed static/dynamic config, the book's vsk.nk claim and field names, submit_at = 0 semantics. Pick ZIP-A or ZIP-G as the base.
- If Keystone-authorized proxy delegation is ever wanted (custody users without a hotkey), the existing ZKP1-to-target flow already binds the Keystone 'To' address to the delegate's VAN. It works only at whole-bundle granularity, shows a raw address, and puts the holder's total ZEC in a memo the delegate can decrypt. Percentage splits on-device need multi-action ZKP1, a new TX1 profile and firmware-aware display.
- Rename the overloaded 'delegation' terms in specs and API: ZKP1 'delegation', proxy delegation, and 'Share Delegation' / 'Server-Delegated Share Submission' (WAPI:603; book delegation/server-delegated-shares.md; data-types.md:104).

## risks_and_holes
- Double-spend hole: if the split proof uses a new nullifier tag or set instead of the ZKP2 VAN nullifier (ZIP-A:332, 447-456), one VAN could be both voted and split.
- Field-wrap hole: the book only requires that 'amounts sum correctly' (delegation-setup.md:15). Without per-output range checks, a prover can mint weight with a negative or overflowed output.
- Authority reset hole: ZKP1 hard-codes MAX_PROPOSAL_AUTHORITY (ZIP-A:703-707). A split that copies that constant instead of the input bitmask would restore authority for already-voted proposals.
- Specs are stale on proposal width (16 bits / 15 in all ZIPs and the book vs 51 bits / 50 in code). A split circuit or new spec text written from the ZIPs would be wrong.
- Delegator observability leak: reused gov_comm_rand (ZIP-A:895-896) plus a delegator who knows the opening lets the delegator track which proposals the delegate voted on and when, by successor-VAN matching. It applies today to custody controllers too. ZIP-A:1470-1480's 'never externally observable' is then false.
- The delegate learns the exact delegated num_ballots (ZKP2 witness). With a 100% split of one bundle, or the Keystone memo in a direct-to-delegate ZKP1 (KS:235-241, encrypted to the delegate inside on-chain tx1_effects), the delegate learns the holder's bundle or total balance. If delivery is via a DM or other identifying channel, the delegate also learns the holder's identity.
- No revocation: once split, the VAN is bound to the delegate's vpk. An unresponsive or malicious delegate makes the weight unusable, and the specs have no reclaim path or timelock.
- Hotkey lifecycle mismatch: ZIP-A requires a fresh, round-only, non-backed-up hotkey (ZIP-A:501-508). A delegate who loses the key mid-round loses all delegated weight, and there is no public-chain recovery for imported VANs (exporting-to-external-software.md:175-195).
- VCT capacity: each delegated VAN is voted separately (2 leaves per proposal). E.g., 10k delegators × 11 VANs × 50 proposals × 2 ≈ 11M leaves vs 2^24 ≈ 16.7M (ZIP-A:1557-1572). Depth 24 may be too small at scale.
- Delegate liveness: V VANs × P proposals of ZKP2 and up to 16 shares each, helper per-helper quotas (book guide.md:668) and expensive ZKP3s (SUB:316-324) mean late delegations can push delegates into single-share mode or lose votes before the deadline.
- Last-moment mode for delegates exposes each delegated VAN's full amount to any decryptor (share-splits.md:134-140). It also creates a burst of same-decision immediate reveals that fingerprints the delegate's aggregate.
- Timing linkage: a split submitted by the delegator's client right after ZKP1 links that bundle's governance nullifiers to its delegate set. Splitting several bundles at once links the bundles together.
- Vote-buying: there is no receipt-freeness claim, deterministic secrets already allow receipts, and proxy delegation makes vote rental trivial and verifiable. The threat model must state this explicitly, especially if delegate receipts are productized.
- Re-delegation (allowed by the book: delegation-setup.md:3,24; delegating-your-vote.md:24) creates unbounded chains, complicates 'did my delegate vote', and enables weight laundering. Policy is undecided.
- Directory spoofing: X-handle linkage is outside every spec. An unauthenticated or spoofed directory entry redirects weight. It needs the same trust-anchor discipline as the signed config (book guide.md:137-150, 664).
- The Keystone memo is unverified, and a handle cannot be shown verifiably on-device. Only the raw 'To' address is bound. Users cannot meaningfully verify '@alice' on Keystone.
- WAPI step 8 assumes the wallet computed van_cmx itself (WAPI:161-164). There is no specified discovery path for a delegate to find incoming VANs, and no VAN-nullifier query endpoint.
- Version gating: bumping vote_protocol for the split circuit makes every non-upgraded wallet MUST-reject the round config (WAPI:719-722), not just wallets that use proxy delegation.
- Book-vs-ZIP contradictions (vsk.nk == nk; governance nullifier arity; signed-note value; single-EA Chaum-Pedersen tally; field names) make the book unusable as a normative base. Gov Steps V1 §6.0, the only 'specified' delegation extension, is unavailable.
- Sole-voter-on-option leak (SUB:549-555) now reveals a delegate's aggregate delegated weight. Influencers publicly announcing votes plus timing could narrow which delegators were involved.
- Share-status queries by delegators (if receipts expose blinds) reveal to helpers which share nullifiers they care about (SUB:542-548).

## open_questions
- Mechanism: a hotkey-signed VAN split after registration (spec direction), a multi-VAN ZKP1 at registration (needs Keystone/TX1 changes), or whole-bundle routing via the existing ZKP1-to-target flow (no circuit change, note-bundle granularity)?
- Split arity: iterated 1→2 transactions (book) or a single 1→(k+1) proof with k ≤ 10? Is k ≤ 10 per split transaction, per VAN, or per holder per round?
- Should delegators be able to see THAT and on WHICH proposals their delegate voted (successor-VAN tracking via reused gov_comm_rand)? Is that a feature or a leak to close by re-randomizing in ZKP2?
- Should Vizor support delegate-published, verifiable vote receipts (revealing shares_hash and decision per VC, optionally blinds), given they also enable vote buying?
- May delegates re-delegate received weight (the book says yes)? If so, to what depth?
- Is revocation or reclaim before the deadline required? If yes, a VAN needs a second authorized key or a timelock, which changes the VAN preimage.
- Is there a per-round delegation cutoff (e.g., before vote_end_time minus the last-moment buffer)? Is it an on-chain round parameter (possibly bound into voting_round_id) or client policy?
- Should per-proposal delegation (disjoint authority bitmasks) be supported in addition to weight splitting?
- Delegate key model: a stable, backed-up delegate hotkey reused across rounds, or a per-round hotkey attested by a long-lived identity key linked to the X account? Which one does the ICNS-style X post commit to?
- Delivery of delegated VAN openings: in-band encrypted ciphertext on the vote chain (trial decryption, chain recovery, delegator anonymity) or an off-chain channel like the custody handoff?
- Who curates and signs the delegate directory (Vizor, the vote manager/endorser, or permissionless with an X-post proof)? Where is it hosted, and which trust anchor signs it?
- Should delegates be able to merge many small delegated VANs into one (fewer proofs and leaves, but large-weight VANs that weaken denomination-band privacy)?
- Rounding policy for percentages in whole ballots, minimum per-delegate amount, and handling of multi-bundle holders (split every bundle vs merge first)?
- Which protocol draft (adam/ZIP-A or greg/ZIP-G) is canonical for the extension? Will the ZIPs be updated to current code (51-bit authority, tx1_effects, 1-zatoshi signed note, batch messages) first?
- Unverified: the content of 'Gov Steps V1 §6.0' (and §3.5), cited by the book and voting-circuits README but absent from all provided checkouts. Can someone supply it?
- Should Keystone (custody) users be able to proxy-delegate without ever holding a phone hotkey (direct ZKP1 to a delegate target)? If so, is a raw-address-only device display plus an unverified memo acceptable, given the memo exposes their total eligible ZEC to the delegate?
